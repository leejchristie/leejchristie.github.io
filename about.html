The short bio

Smart, with the house lights up.

Lee Christie leads People & Organisation for around 800 people at a global life sciences company, after eight years in workforce analytics, talent and HR leadership at a global consultancy.

He builds AI and analytics tools in his day job, studies organisational psychology, and speaks about AI, ethics and change for executives, universities and professional bodies. The talks are practical, evidence-led and honest about the awkward bit.
